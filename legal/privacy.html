Privacy Policy

Your Family's Privacy Matters

Last updated: March 28, 2026  ·  Effective: March 28, 2026

Short version: We collect only what's needed to run the app. We never sell your data. Your children's information stays within your family. You can delete everything at any time.

1. Who We Are

Kindo ("we", "us", or "our") is a family chore and reward application. The controller of your personal data is Kindo. Contact us at privacy@kindo.app.

2.2 Payment Information

If you enable real-money rewards, payments are processed by Stripe, Inc. Your full card number is entered directly into Stripe's secure form and never touches Kindo's servers. We store only:

  • Last 4 digits of the card (for display only)
  • Card brand (e.g., Visa)
  • A Stripe customer ID (a reference token, not card data)

2.3 Subscription Information

Premium subscriptions are processed through the Apple App Store or Google Play. We do not store your payment method for subscriptions — this is handled entirely by Apple and Google.

2.4 Technical Information

We collect minimal technical data: crash reports (via Sentry, if enabled) and anonymised performance metrics. No advertising identifiers (IDFA, GAID) are collected.

3. Children's Privacy (COPPA)

Kindo is a family app designed for parents and children, including children under 13. We comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

  • Parental consent is required before any child data is collected. The parent (account holder) provides this consent during setup.
  • We collect only children's first names / nicknames and chore records — the minimum needed for the app to function.
  • Children's data is not shared with third parties, used for advertising, or processed for any purpose beyond running Kindo within your family.
  • Parents can delete all data (including children's data) at any time via Parent Settings → Reset All Data.
  • We do not send marketing communications to children.

4. How We Use Your Information

  • Provide, maintain, and improve the Kindo app
  • Authenticate parents and secure the Parent Zone
  • Process quest approvals and payment rewards
  • Send quest completion / approval notifications (if enabled)
  • Diagnose crashes and fix bugs (anonymous crash reports)

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose beyond running Kindo.

5. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA) or United Kingdom, our legal bases for processing are:

  • Contract performance — processing necessary to provide the Kindo service you signed up for
  • Legitimate interests — crash reporting and fraud prevention
  • Consent — push notifications and optional features
  • Legal obligation — where processing is required by applicable law

6. Data Storage and Security

  • Data is stored on Supabase (PostgreSQL cloud database) servers located in the United States. Supabase complies with GDPR and has appropriate data transfer safeguards.
  • Your PIN is stored as a SHA-256 hash with a unique salt — it cannot be reversed or read, even by us.
  • Your family ID is stored in encrypted secure storage on your device.
  • All data in transit is encrypted with TLS 1.2 or higher.
  • Row Level Security (RLS) ensures families can only access their own data.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you have the right to:

  • Access — request a copy of the data we hold about you
  • Rectification — correct inaccurate data via the app or by contacting us
  • Erasure — delete all your family's data via Parent Settings → Reset All Data, or contact us
  • Portability — receive your data in a machine-readable format
  • Objection / Restriction — object to or restrict certain processing
  • Withdraw consent — for notification permissions at any time via device settings

To exercise any right, contact privacy@kindo.app. We respond within 30 days.

9. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. When you delete your data via the app, all family data (profiles, tasks, transactions) is permanently deleted from our servers within 30 days. Stripe retains payment records as required by financial regulations (typically 7 years).

10. International Transfers

Your data may be processed in the United States. For EEA/UK residents, transfers are covered by Standard Contractual Clauses with our sub-processors.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. We'll notify you of material changes via the app or email. Continued use after changes constitutes acceptance.
